Queenswood Cottage Restaurant

International & Local Cuisine

The Queenswood Restaurant offers a delightful culinary experience in the heart of Nuwara Eliya. Our restaurant serves an exquisite selection of international and local cuisine, prepared with the finest ingredients and traditional cooking methods. Located just 2789 feet from Nuwara Eliya town center, a pleasant 5-minute walk away.

Our menu features authentic Italian, Indian, European, and Asian dishes, all crafted by our experienced chefs. We take pride in accommodating various dietary preferences with dedicated vegan and gluten-free options. All our food is prepared following halal standards.

Open daily for lunch and dinner until 10:00 PM, we welcome both hotel guests and visitors from Nuwara Eliya and beyond. Whether you’re looking for a hearty breakfast, a leisurely lunch, or an elegant dinner, our restaurant provides the perfect setting.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Do you accept walk-ins?

Yes, we welcome walk-in guests! While reservations are recommended for dinner and larger groups, we always do our best to accommodate everyone. If you're in the area, feel free to stop by.

Is the restaurant family-friendly?

Absolutely. We have a dedicated kids' menu, highchairs available, and a warm, welcoming atmosphere suitable for families with children of all ages.

Can you accommodate large groups?

Yes, we can host groups and private events. We recommend contacting us in advance to make arrangements and ensure the best possible experience for your party.

What are your busiest hours?

Our busiest times are typically dinner hours from 7:00 PM to 9:00 PM. For a more relaxed experience, lunch or an early dinner is a great option. You can also use our booking feature to secure your preferred time.

Do you offer takeaway or delivery?

Yes, we offer takeaway services for all menu items. Simply call us or visit the restaurant to place your order. We also work with local delivery partners in Nuwara Eliya, so please ask when calling for available delivery options in your area.

What local Sri Lankan dishes do you serve?

We specialize in authentic Sri Lankan cuisine including Kottu Roti, fragrant Rice & Curry, Hoppers (Appa), String Hoppers, and spicy Pol Sambol. Our chefs prepare these traditional dishes using local recipes and fresh ingredients. Ask our staff for daily chef specials featuring regional favorites from the hill country.

Do you offer vegetarian or vegan options?

Yes, we offer a variety of vegetarian dishes and can prepare several items vegan upon request. Sri Lankan cuisine naturally includes many plant-based ingredients such as lentils, vegetables, coconut, and spices, allowing us to provide flavorful options for vegetarian and vegan guests.

Is parking available at the restaurant?

Yes, parking is available for guests visiting our restaurant. If you're staying at Queenswood Cottage, you can conveniently walk to the restaurant area. Our staff will also be happy to guide you to available parking spaces when you arrive.
