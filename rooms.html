Questions

Frequently Asked

How do I reserve a room?

Book directly on our website using the “Book Now” button, call +94 522 222 000, or email queenswoodcottage@gmail.com with your dates. Our reservation team is ready to assist you 24 hours a day.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Rooms are ready for occupancy at 02.00 PM and we ask guests to check out by 11.00 AM. If your travel plans need adjusted times, please tell us when booking and we will accommodate when feasible.

Is an airport shuttle provided?

We don’t run our own transport service, but our concierge can arrange airport transfers with trusted providers. Contact us before your arrival so we can organise this for you.

Is parking available?

Yes, secure on-site parking is available for all guests. A small daily fee is charged, and full pricing is shared when you make your reservation.

Can I request a particular view or room position?

Certainly. When reserving, share any preference about room location or view, or ask for rooms close together if you are travelling as a group. We will assign the best available option based on occupancy.

What dining is available, and what does it cost?

Our on-site restaurant serves local Sri Lankan dishes and simple international meals. Most meals are priced between LKR 1,000 and LKR 2,000 per person, with beverages and snacks usually under LKR 500.
